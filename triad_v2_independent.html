Our mission

To help build communities where safety, dignity, and support are non-negotiable.

TRIAD promotes awareness, safer public spaces, survivor-centred support, and responsible community partnerships. We are an independent community initiative, not law enforcement; concerns about suspected offences belong with appropriate authorities.

03 / Support hub

Information can
open a door.

If someone is unsafe, the priority is immediate safety and support from trusted people and qualified services.

✦

Immediate danger

Move to a safer public place if possible and contact local emergency services or someone you trust. Do not confront a dangerous person.

✦

Someone wants to talk

Listen calmly, avoid blame, respect their choices, and do not pressure them to share details.

✦

Finding professional help

Seek qualified medical, counselling, legal, or safeguarding support through reputable local organizations.

✦

Privacy matters

Protect privacy: do not publish names, photos, addresses, allegations, or personal stories without informed consent and appropriate guidance.

Important: This website is an informational community initiative, not an emergency service, confidential counselling service, or official reporting channel. Do not submit incident reports or identifying details here.
